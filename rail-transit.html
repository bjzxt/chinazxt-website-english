Rail Transit

Advanced Train Transparent Components Manufacturing

Overview

Our Rail Transit sector specializes in the development, production, and sales of transparent components for rail transit trains. We are a leading manufacturer in this specialized field, providing high-quality transparent solutions for various types of rail vehicles including metro trains, light rail vehicles, and high-speed trains.

Our Products

Train Windows

High-quality transparent windows for passenger trains, featuring excellent clarity, durability, and safety standards. Our windows meet all railway industry requirements for impact resistance and optical quality.

  • Passenger compartment windows
  • Cabin windows
  • Emergency exit windows
  • Custom-shaped windows

Windshield Assemblies

Advanced windshield systems for locomotive cabs and driver compartments, engineered for optimal visibility, safety, and environmental protection.

  • Locomotive windshields
  • Driver cab windows
  • Heated windshield options
  • Anti-reflective coatings

Interior Partitions

Transparent interior partitions and panels for passenger compartments, providing functionality while maintaining modern aesthetic standards.

  • Compartment dividers
  • Door panels
  • Partitions with integrated displays
  • Custom interior solutions

Specialized Components

Custom transparent components for special rail applications, meeting specific operational and safety requirements.

  • Skylights and roof panels
  • Observation deck windows
  • Emergency access panels
  • Special purpose glazing
