Rail Transit

Advanced Train Transparent Components Manufacturing

Overview

Our Rail Transit sector specializes in the development, production, and sale of transparent components for all rail transit vehicles. We are a leading manufacturer in this specialized field, providing high-quality transparent solutions for various types of rail vehicles, including metro trains, light rail vehicles, and high-speed trains.

Our Products

Train Windows

Our high-quality transparent windows for passenger trains feature excellent clarity, durability, and safety. All windows meet stringent railway industry requirements for impact resistance and safety.

  • Passenger compartment windows
  • Cabin windows
  • Emergency exit windows
  • Custom-shaped windows

Windshield Assemblies

Our advanced windshield systems for locomotive cabs and driver compartments deliver maximum visibility, reliability, safety, and environmental protection.

  • Locomotive windshields
  • Driver cab windows
  • Heated windshield options
  • Anti-reflective coatings

Interior Partitions

Our transparent interior partitions and panels for passenger compartments meet strict aesthetic standards while delivering privacy and modern design.

  • Compartment dividers
  • Door panels
  • Partitions with displays
  • Custom interior solutions

Specialized Components

Our custom transparent components for specialized rail applications are engineered to meet specific operational and safety requirements.

  • Skylights and roof panels
  • Observation deck windows
  • Emergency access panels
  • Special purpose glazing
