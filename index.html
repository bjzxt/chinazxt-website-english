About Zhongxingtong Group

Founded in 1994 by seven founders, Zhongxingtong Group is headquartered in Beijing Zhongguancun Software Park. After 30 years of development, we have grown into a diversified enterprise group with five core business sectors. Through 30 years of hard work and consistent business strategies, we have accumulated strong capital strength and built an excellent team of integrity, capability, and responsibility.
