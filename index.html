About Zhongxingtong Group

Established in 1994, Zhongxingtong Group is headquartered in Beijing Zhongguancun Software Park. Over three decades of development, the Group has evolved into a diversified enterprise with five core business segments. Through sustained strategic development and operational excellence, the Group has built strong capital capabilities and established a professional team characterized by integrity, expertise, and accountability.
