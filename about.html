About Zhongxingtong Group

30 Years of Excellence and Innovation

Group Profile

Established in 1994, Zhongxingtong Group is headquartered in Beijing Zhongguancun Software Park. Over three decades of development, the Group has evolved into a diversified enterprise with five core business segments: Tax Technology & Digital Solutions, Equity Investment, Rail Transit, Binggouhe Scenic Spot, and Industrial Real Estate.

Through sustained strategic development and operational excellence, the Group has built strong capital capabilities and established a professional team characterized by integrity, expertise, and accountability.
