About Zhongxingtong Group

30 Years of Excellence and Innovation

Group Profile

Zhongxingtong Group was founded in 1994 by seven founders and is headquartered in Beijing Zhongguancun Software Park. After 30 years of development, we have grown into a diversified enterprise group with five core business sectors: Finance & Taxation Informatization, Equity Investment, Rail Transit, Binggouhe Scenic Spot, and Industrial Real Estate.

Through 30 years of arduous struggle and consistent business strategies based on diligence and thrift, the Group has accumulated strong capital strength. We have built an excellent team characterized by integrity, capability, and responsibility.
